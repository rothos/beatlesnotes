Notes on … every Beatles song

Between May 1989 and February 2000 the musicologist Alan W. Pollack wrote a close analysis of every song the Beatles released: its key, its form, its chords and the small surprises that make it work. This is all {{ n_essays }} of his notes, re-set for reading.

New to music theory? Start with the first 28

Pollack’s first notes, starting with “We Can Work It Out”, double as a short course in the ideas the rest of the series relies on: harmonic rhythm, the V-of-V, cross relations, phrasing. Read them in order. The glossary fills in the terms.

Go album by album

Every single, EP and LP from “Love Me Do” to “Let It Be”, with each track linked to its note.

Find a song

Sort and filter every song in the canon by key, meter, year, album or recording date.

Follow the recording sessions

Tuomas Eerola’s chronology groups the songs into twelve recording projects, from September 1962 to January 1970.
